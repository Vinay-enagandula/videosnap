Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Click on the Signup button, fill in your details and submit the registration form.

I forgot my password.

Click the "Forgot Password" option on the login page and follow the reset instructions.

How do I change my email?

Go to Profile → Settings → Edit Profile to update your email address.

How do I delete my account?

Navigate to Settings and choose Delete Account. This action is permanent.
